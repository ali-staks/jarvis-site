Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner on their own Mac.

  • What it accesses: with the owner's Google sign-in, it reads, labels, drafts and sends the owner's Gmail, and reads and edits the owner's Google Calendar, only to do what the owner asks.
  • Where data is kept: on the owner's Mac. Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Text is sent to an AI model provider only to answer the owner's own requests.
  • Removing access: sign out in Jarvis, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
